Field guide

Know your scope. Know your tools.

Permission is the first packet.

ReconDeck wraps two well-known tools. It does not grant authorization. Only scan systems you own or have explicit, documented permission to test.

Discovery sends ARP or ICMP probes to an entire range. Port scans touch services and may appear in remote logs.

Attack surface, not exploitation

Safe audit runs a curated, non-intrusive NSE set for banners, HTTP metadata, TLS certificates, SSH host keys, and SMB identity. ReconDeck intentionally does not include exploit execution, credential attacks, brute force, or denial-of-service features.

Unlock ARP and OS detection

ARP capture and OS fingerprinting need raw packet access. Run the launcher with suitable privileges, or grant the two binaries the narrower capability and restart ReconDeck.

sudo setcap cap_net_raw,cap_net_admin+eip /usr/bin/nmap sudo setcap cap_net_raw,cap_net_admin+eip /usr/sbin/arp-scan

In a restricted container, local capabilities may not be enough. In that case, Auto uses Nmap's unprivileged ping discovery and TCP connect scanning.
